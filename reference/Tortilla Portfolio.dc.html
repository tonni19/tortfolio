01 / ABOUT

Hi, I'm Tortilla — a 2D animator and digital artist making characters that refuse to sit still.

I draw people, animals and the odd sentient matchstick. Most of my work starts as a scribble in a sketchbook and ends as a loop — hand-drawn frame by frame, coloured loud, animated with a bit of a swagger.

Commissions, character sheets, promo loops, comics. If it moves and it has a face, I want to draw it.
